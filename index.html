Plan a deload week
close
A deload is a planned lighter week — usually about 10-20% less weight and/or fewer hard sets — taken every 4-8 weeks of consistent training. It gives connective tissue and your nervous system time to catch up with the muscle adaptations you've already earned, which is generally thought to lower overuse-injury risk and can help push through a stall that isn't responding to changes on a single exercise. It's not mandatory, and skipping one occasionally is fine — but going many months without one is usually when plateaus and nagging aches start to stack up.
Starting one suggests weights about 10% lighter across every exercise for the next 7 days. Nothing about your logged history changes — it only affects the suggested working weight.
